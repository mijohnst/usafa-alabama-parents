Why a Saber?

Cadets traditionally carry a ceremonial saber during formal ceremonies and graduation — a handcrafted, personalized symbol of everything they've earned across four demanding years at the Academy. It's not required, but for the families who choose one, it becomes one of the most treasured keepsakes of their cadet's journey.

We're raising funds so every one of Alabama's 20 Class of 2027 cadets can have one, regardless of what any individual family can contribute on their own. This is a yearly tradition for our club — we run this fundraiser every year for each new graduating class of Alabama cadets.

$500 × 20 cadets = $10,000 goal
See the saber on USAFA's official shop →

Personalized sabers take 4–6 weeks to produce, which is why we're asking for gifts by January 15, 2027 — well ahead of graduation.
